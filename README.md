# FuseBox Detective

A small, mobile-friendly, safety-first webpage for recording which light, switch, or appliance appears to be associated with an electrical trip.

## Live website

https://jazblue.github.io/fusebox-detective/

## Features

- Guided questions about when the trip happens and what loses power
- A record of observations for hallway, stairway, landing, and other lights
- Safety warnings for burning smells, crackling, heat, scorch marks, damp, or electric shocks
- A copyable summary to share with a qualified electrician
- Responsive layout and no external libraries or tracking

## Run locally

Open `index.html` in a modern browser. The page is static and does not send form entries to a server.

## Electrical safety

This tool is an observation checklist, not a diagnosis or electrical test. Do not remove fuse-box covers, open fittings, touch wiring, bypass safety devices, or repeatedly reset a tripping breaker. Stop testing if warning signs are present. If a light or circuit appears to trigger a trip, leave it unused and arrange for a suitably qualified electrician to investigate. For smoke, fire, or immediate danger, move to safety and contact emergency services.

## Technology

HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. No build step required.
